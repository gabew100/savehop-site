SaveHop

SaveHop keeps your saves for DRM-free Windows games in step across a Windows PC, a Steam Deck and an Android phone running GameNative.

When you start a game through SaveHop, it downloads the newest save from cloud storage you already use, such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. When you stop playing, it uploads your save again, so the next device you pick up starts where you left off.

SaveHop runs on your own devices. There's no SaveHop account and no SaveHop server: your saves go straight between your devices and your storage. With Google Drive, SaveHop can only see the files it creates, not the rest of your Drive.

SaveHop is in early testing and isn't available for download yet.
